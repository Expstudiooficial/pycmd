# Board

A place for plans and notes that you arrange instead of scroll.

## Cards

- **+ Note**, **+ Heading**, **+ To-do**, **+ Image**, **+ Link** add a card where there is room.
- Double-click the board (double-tap on a phone) to drop a note right there.
- Double-click a card (tap it twice) to write in it. Escape or a click outside stops.
- In a to-do list, Enter makes the next item; tick the box when it is done.
- **Paste** an image or a link straight onto the board.

## Arranging

- Drag a card to move it, and drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.
- Pick a colour while a card is selected.
- **Connect:** drag a card's green dot onto another card, or press Connect and tap two cards.
- Double-click an arrow to give it a label. Select it and press Delete to remove it.
- Drag the background to move around. Zoom with the wheel, a pinch, or − + and **Fit**.

## Boards

Make as many as you like with **New**. Rename one by typing in the name box. **Delete** asks twice.
Everything saves itself; **Ctrl+Z** undoes and **Ctrl+Y** redoes.
**Export .md** writes the board to `boards/<name>.md` in your workspace: cards, lists and arrows.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| N | a new note |
| Enter | write in the selected card |
| Delete | delete what is selected |
| Ctrl+D | duplicate the selected card |
| Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y | undo / redo |

## Console

`note <text>` drops a note on your board. `note list` shows your boards.
